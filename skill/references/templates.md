# Output contracts

## Transcript
Timestamp | original speech | faithful English | uncertainty.
Then English script alone when requested. Put factual flags and proposed editorial changes separately.

## Scene plan
Scene / source interval | generation seconds | exact dialogue | entry prop state | action | exit prop state | camera / reference.
Separate source endpoint and output duration. 10+8+6+6+6+8+4=48 is one example, not a mandatory count or a timed speech guarantee.

## Delivery
**Scene 01 — 8 seconds**
Display actual accepted 9:16 image here.
Then a single copy-ready block containing:
- Duration, vertical format and supplied image as first frame.
- Specific character/set/prop states to preserve.
- Exact approved dialogue.
- On “[words]”: which hand performs which action on which object.
- Precise ending pose/state.
- Full unchanged VOICE — ELIAS block.
- Purposeful camera direction, room sound, no extra speaker/text/music unless requested.

Do not separate all images from their prompts or invent a missing image link. Do not treat a corrected frame as an extra scene.

## Audit
Clip/version; duration/dimensions; expected vs actual words; speech end; identity/prop/contact and adjacent-state issues; evidence checked; audio/playback aspects unchecked; keep/trim/regenerate; targeted fix.

## Checkpoint
Source/hash; source cutoff; translation mode/version; target runtime; voice reference; scene states; accepted asset versions; prompts delivered; clips reviewed; pending work; superseded versions.
Keep private source media locators out of public handoffs.
